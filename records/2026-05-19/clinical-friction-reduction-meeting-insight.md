# Clinical Friction Reduction Meeting Insight

Status: dated meeting insight

Date captured: 2026-05-20

Meeting context: this records an additional 2026-05-19 Health Taiwan deep-cultivation meeting insight attributed to a metabolism physician's comment.

## Core Insight

The physician's point was that Health Taiwan deep-cultivation should not be treated as a normal research project that asks clinical staff to do more work for the sake of a study.

The purpose should be closer to:

```text
use smart healthcare, equipment, workflow tooling, or digital support
to reduce the existing burden on physicians, nurses, and clinical staff
```

not:

```text
ask physicians and nurses to change their workflow, label data, fill extra forms,
or participate in a new research burden just to support an AI team
```

## Why This Matters

If a proposed AI system increases clicks, training, supervision, exception handling, or cross-system switching, it may fail even if the model is technically good.

The Health Taiwan proposal should therefore make this principle explicit:

```text
The project must reduce clinical friction and workforce burden.
It should not transfer research or operational burden onto already busy clinical staff.
```

## Proposal Implication

For this urology previsit system, the deep-cultivation proposal should emphasize:

- workflow burden reduction
- cognitive load reduction
- documentation assistance
- previsit information compression
- clinician-reviewable summary
- nursing workflow support
- low-friction integration
- minimal change to existing clinical behavior

The proposal should not imply:

- clinicians become AI labelers
- nurses must complete every missing field
- physicians must learn a separate complex platform
- the hospital must change workflow mainly to accommodate AI
- research participation is the primary value of the system

## Non-Decision

This insight does not expand scope into production deployment, HIS/EMR integration, autonomous triage, diagnosis, or treatment recommendation.

It strengthens the current accepted direction:

```text
泌尿科門診前問診與醫師覆核摘要支持系統
```

by adding the explicit adoption criterion:

```text
Does this reduce friction for medical staff without creating hidden work?
```


CRM-ready handoff, CRM KPI, CRM budget, CRM procurement, patient messaging,
dashboard, maintenance, or CRM-team coordination.

## Budget Position

Use NT$10,000,000 as the provisional three-year working ceiling for AI 問診 and
physician-review summary until the parent proposal owner or budget owner revises
it.

KPI should not wait until the final budget is confirmed. The fast sequence is:

1. confirm the provisional ceiling;
2. define deliverables and workflow value;
3. define KPI that prove those deliverables;
4. map each budget line to KPI, owner, evidence, and annual checkpoint;
5. revise formal accounting categories later with the budget owner.

# Design Philosophy

## Core Belief

The safest first version is not the smartest system. It is the clearest system.

The purpose is to support a clinical conversation, not replace it.

## First Principles

A previsit system should:

- reduce repeated questioning
- improve readiness before the physician enters
- make missing information visible
- support patients with different abilities
- preserve clinician judgment
- avoid medical overclaiming
- minimize privacy risk

## Design Values

### Restraint

The system should do less, but do it clearly.

### Transparency

Every output should be understandable by a patient, nurse, or physician.

### Human Review

The clinician remains the final interpreter.

### Low Friction

The system should fit into waiting-room or nurse-assisted workflow without requiring a new operational culture.

### Accessibility

Older adults, low-literacy users, and people needing language or staff support are part of the default user model.

## Rejected Design Instinct

Do not start by asking, "How can AI interview the patient?"

Start by asking, "What repeated information does the clinic wish it already had before the physician enters?"
